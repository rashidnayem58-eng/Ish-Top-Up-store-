Frequently asked questions

Important information before placing an order.

How do I submit an order?

Add your package to the cart, fill in the required information and tap the email submission button. Your email app will open with the order details.

How do I pay?

Select bKash or Nagad, use the displayed payment number, and enter the transaction ID in the checkout form.

Is payment automatically verified?

No. This website version only collects the transaction ID in the email draft. Payment verification must be done separately.

Why is my email app opening?

The website uses a mail link to prepare your order. You need an email app configured on your device and must send the email yourself.
